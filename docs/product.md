# LocalSignal MVP Product Spec

## Positioning

AI-powered local change intelligence.

## First Market

- Fort Lee
- Edgewater
- Palisades Park

## MVP Promise

Every week, show the most meaningful local changes:

- rising places
- keyword spikes
- sentiment shifts
- cross-region attention
- emerging neighborhood patterns

## Primary Screen

`This Week Nearby`

Each signal card should answer:

- what changed?
- what evidence supports it?
- why might a local person care?

## Success Metric

Can the product surface 10 weekly signals that a local person would save, share, or talk about?
